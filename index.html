How to Play

Pamper Time is 20 gentle mini-games — skincare, make-up, tidying and pet care. There is no timer and no way to lose. Relax and finish each mini at your own pace.

🫧 Clean / Wash: drag your finger or mouse over every bubble, speck or tangle.

👆 Tap: tap every glowing dot, drop or bubble.

✋ Drag & place: drag each item onto its matching outline.

🔢 In order: tap the numbered steps 1 → 2 → 3…

🎨 Paint: drag to paint the whole area until it sparkles.

Finish a mini to earn up to 3 stars. A wrong tap just wobbles — nothing is lost. Use the ♪ button for sound and II to pause.

Choose a Mini

Tap an unlocked mini — finish one to unlock the next.
